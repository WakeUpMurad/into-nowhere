The owner again reported no payments. YouTube Studio's Shorts table showed 66 views on the original dark video, 55 on the light video, 63 on Center Challenge and 16 on Anti-Flex (200 in total). Instagram's public Reel cards showed 77, 24, 15 and 24 respectively. Both profiles still had zero subscribers/followers. These are platform view counters, not verified organic viewers, website visits or sales.

The latest YouTube dashboard also showed an anomalous **33,870.9% average percentage viewed** for Anti-Flex and 2.5 channel watch hours. Four previously opened public video tabs were found playing on repeat during verification. That checking mistake may have contaminated watch duration and other counters, including earlier figures; its exact contribution cannot be determined. Playback was paused through the visible UI, pause state was checked, and those tabs were navigated to the channel/profile grids to prevent further looping. Do not use the duration or retention figures above as evidence of audience interest or forecast sales.

There is still no measured website-to-checkout funnel or independently retrieved payment-order total. Further creative variants alone do not resolve the untested paid value and funded-TON-wallet requirement. ## Upload checklist

1. Create a separate project channel; preserve the existing personal channel.
2. Add the actual site as the first website link and verify it opens.
3. Upload the original vertical video with its original procedural soundtrack. Keep the payment-method disclosure and mobile-safe text visible.
4. Use the description above and a public post, with truthful audience settings. Do not invent an American operator location or customers.
5. Verify the published video, channel and website link and record their real URLs here. Use actual published links rather than an unverified proposed handle.

YouTube Shorts descriptions/comments have non-clickable URLs, so the video CTA points to the channel profile. [Official link rules](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en), [profile website links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2657964?hl=en).

Vertical or square videos up to three minutes can be Shorts. [Official upload instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12779649?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en).

Use the 2560×1440 banner with important text inside its centered safe area; maximum file size is 6 MB. [Official banner guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en).

Do not use fake testimonials, purchased engagement, repeated unsolicited comments, or promises of financial, health or romantic results. The 25% promise describes allocation from actual received gross payments, not already completed aid. [YouTube spam and scam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en).
